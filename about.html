About me

Hello every!

Welcome to my personal website on the vast World Wide Web. Since you’ve made your way to this section, it means you’re interested in learning a bit more about me =). Here, you’ll get to know me a little better.

On the internet they usually call me Artemon. I’m an aspiring systems programmer and an indie game developer (already active in that field). I have quite a lot of interests, but most often I program, write soundtracks, play games and listen to music.

I know how to program in C#, Python, GDScript, and a bit of C. Currently, I am learning HTML and CSS. I plan to thoroughly study C++ or Rust and Assembly.

For the record, I’m a beginner programmer; I only got interested in this about three years ago, and ever since then, I’ve been working toward my goal of becoming a skilled programmer. While I previously just explored various areas (Python, WebDev, GameDev, AI, software, etc.), I’m now diving into the field headfirst.

I'm also a game developer. I used to make games in Unity, but later switched to Godot. I primarily focus on 2D games, though for my latest project, I decided to try my hand at 3D. I haven’t released any "finished" games yet—only small prototypes. If you’d like, you can play them here.
